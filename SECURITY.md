# Reporting a security problem

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability in Meow or its licence server.

Email **hello@qfiber.co.il** with "Security" in the subject, or use the
[contact form](https://meow.qfiber.co.il/contact). Include the Meow version, your
operating system and the steps to reproduce it. We will reply and keep you informed
until it is fixed.

How Meow protects calls and credentials: [meow.qfiber.co.il/security](https://meow.qfiber.co.il/security).
